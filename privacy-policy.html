Privacy Policy for Be Still

Last updated: September 5 2026

Be Still is a personal prayer app. This page explains what information the app does and does not collect.

Information We Collect

Be Still does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account, sign-in, or registration of any kind. We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services within the app.

Locally Stored Content

Some audio recordings used in the app (such as chaplets or devotions) are bundled directly within the app and play entirely on your device. No data related to this playback is collected or sent anywhere.

Third-Party Content

Be Still displays certain content from third-party websites and services directly within the app, including:

  • Daily Mass readings from the United States Conference of Catholic Bishops (USCCB) website
  • Video content embedded from YouTube

When this content loads, your device communicates directly with these third-party services in order to display it. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies, which are outside of our control. We encourage you to review:

  • USCCB Privacy Policy
  • Google/YouTube Privacy Policy

Video content is loaded using YouTube's privacy-enhanced ("no cookie") embedding mode where possible, which limits the use of tracking cookies until a video is actually played.

Children's Privacy

Be Still does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Since the app collects no personal data at all, this applies equally regardless of the user's age.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a revised "Last updated" date.
